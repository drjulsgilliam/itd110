The red-pen notes

Numbers match the pins on the page above.
  1. 1

    The purple-to-blue gradient

    Violet-into-blue with blurred blobs is the single most common AI default. It signals "template" before anyone reads a word.

    FixChoose colors from the brand or the subject's own world.

  2. 2

    A headline that says nothing

    "Elevate your workflow" fits a calendar, a CRM or a coffee machine. After reading it you still don't know what Nexora does.

    FixSay what the product does, for whom: "Shared timesheets for design studios."

  3. 3

    Buzzword soup

    Unlock, seamless, intuitive, streamline, drive growth. Each word is filler the reader has learned to skip.

    FixConcrete nouns and numbers you can actually back up.

  4. 4

    Vague, competing buttons

    "Get Started" and "Learn More" don't say what happens next. Get started doing what?

    FixOne primary action with a specific label: "Try it with your team's data."

  5. 5

    A fake dashboard as the hero

    Grey bars and a purple squiggle stand in for a product that doesn't exist. Every AI hero has one.

    FixShow a real screenshot, a real photo or a live demo.

  6. 6

    Everything centered

    Centered heading, centered paragraph, centered cards, centered CTA band. The page has no left edge and no reading rhythm.

    FixLeft-align body copy and vary the layout from section to section.

  7. 7

    Emoji as icons

    🚀 🔒 ⚡ render differently on every OS and say nothing specific about the feature.

    FixA consistent icon set, or no icons at all.

  8. 8

    The interchangeable three cards

    Fast, secure, integrates. Swap the titles between cards, or onto a competitor's site, and nothing changes.

    FixFeatures only this product has, each with a specific detail.

  9. 9

    Made-up stats

    10K+, 99.9%, 24/7, 150+. Round numbers with no source for a product with no users.

    FixOnly real figures, with where they come from.

  10. 10

    Placeholder logo wall

    "Trusted by" Acme, Globex and Initech: fictional companies from cartoons, left in by the generator.

    FixReal customers with permission, or leave the section out.

  11. 11

    Generic testimonials

    Five stars, first name and initial, "game-changer". Publishing invented reviews as real is also deceptive, and illegal in many places.

    FixReal quotes from real people, with their consent.

  12. 12

    Same card treatment everywhere

    One radius, one shadow and one hover lift on features, stats and quotes alike. When everything is a card, nothing stands out.

    FixReserve borders and shadows for the elements that need them.

  13. 13

    Inter, by default

    A fine typeface, chosen because nobody chose. Combined with the rest, it gives the page no personality.

    FixPick type that fits the subject, with a deliberate display/body pairing.

  14. 14

    A footer full of dead links

    Twelve links to Careers, Press, Changelog and Docs pages that don't exist, plus unlabeled social icons.

    FixLink only to real pages, and label every link.

The takeaway for students: AI doesn't produce broken pages anymore; it produces average ones. The structure is fine. What's missing is everything that requires knowing the subject: real content, a point of view, and design decisions made on purpose.
